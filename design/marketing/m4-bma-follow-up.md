## Ask list (attach to the note)

**1. Pilot districts** (our proposal; open to the BMA's view)
- Phaya Thai (Ari), Ratchathewi (Victory Monument), Bang Rak (Charoen Krung), Khlong Toei and Watthana (Benjakitti, Phrom Phong)
- Phra Nakhon for City Quests
- A district champion contact in each, if the BMA has one

**2. Venues**
- Parks (Lumphini, Benjakitti, Chatuchak), public libraries, district halls, and TCDC if the BMA can introduce us
- For each: whether small groups of 4 to 6 may meet there, opening hours, any booking step, an indoor fallback for rain or PM2.5
- Loy Krathong go-together meeting points, if that moment goes ahead

**3. Moderators and roles** (see `design/reference/staff-access.md`)
- Named BMA staff for the `moderator` role (report queue), and who gets `insight_viewer` (totals of 10 or more only)
- A rota during event hours on pilot weekends
- Confirm: staff never see romance data or private choices; every action is logged

**4. Channel approvals**
- Whether BMA district pages, BMA LINE or VisitBangkok may share the pilot table list or promo 05
- Whose sign-off each post needs, and how long that takes
- The only line used: "แอปอิสระ มีกรุงเทพมหานครเป็นเจ้าภาพและผู้กำกับดูแล / An independent app, hosted and overseen by the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration". No logo, no endorsement, nothing that reads as a BMA product.
