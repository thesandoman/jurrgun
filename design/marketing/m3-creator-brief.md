# M3 · Creator brief


*Marketing, 2026-10-26. For 6 to 8 nano and micro creators (1k to 50k followers) who are newcomers to Bangkok themselves. Dates are relative to launch day (L). The fee and the number of creators wait on the owner's budget decision (launch plan section 09).*

## Jurrgun in one breath

> Jurrgun (เจอกัน) พาคนไปนั่งโต๊ะเล็ก ๆ 4 ถึง 6 คน ในที่จริงของกรุงเทพฯ มีเจ้าภาพโต๊ะดูแล หลังจากนั้นแต่ละคนเลือกเองว่าอยากเจอใครอีก จะรู้ก็ต่อเมื่อเลือกตรงกัน
> Jurrgun puts people at a small table of 4 to 6 at a real Bangkok place, with a Table Host. Afterwards, each person chooses who they'd like to meet again. You only hear back if it's mutual.

**It is:** a way to make friends in person. Small groups, real places, a host at every table.
**It isn't:** a dating app, an events listing or a feed. Dating exists only as a private opt-in (💘 เปิดใจมากกว่าเพื่อน / Open to something more). Don't lead with it, and don't film it.

## What we'd ask you to do

1. Book one table on your own, as a normal member, before L.
2. Go, sit, have a real time. Phone away at the table.
3. Post one piece (reel, TikTok or carousel) **on or after L, at the time we agree**. Not before.

## Embargo

- Nothing about Jurrgun goes public before **L, at the agreed hour**. That includes stories, close friends lists and "something's coming" teasers.
- If the launch date moves, the embargo moves with it. We'll tell you at least 48 hours ahead.

## Must-haves in every post

- **Disclosure:** `#ad` or `#ร่วมกับJurrgun` in the caption, and the platform's paid-partnership label where it exists.
- **The tagline:** กลุ่มเล็ก สถานที่จริง แล้วเจอกันนะ · Small groups. Real Bangkok places. See you there.
- **Your link:** `jurrgun.apps.sv-academy.org/new?src=creator`. Every creator uses this same link. We don't track anyone individually, so there is no personal code.
- **Friends first.** Talk about making friends, a table and a city that felt bigger. Not dates.
- **Facts-only safety, if you mention it at all:** "มีเจ้าภาพโต๊ะทุกโต๊ะ เช็กอินที่ร้าน และรายงานได้ทุกเมื่อ / A Table Host at every table, check-in at the venue, and reporting any time." Don't say "safe", "100% safe" or "verified people".
- **If you mention the BMA,** use this line exactly: 🏛️ แอปอิสระ มีกรุงเทพมหานครเป็นเจ้าภาพและผู้กำกับดูแล / An independent app, hosted and overseen by the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration.

## App footage

- If any app screen appears, **every frame** that shows it carries the on-screen label **"ข้อมูลตัวอย่างสมมติ · Fictional demo profiles" (cuts with a demo chat: "ข้อมูลและบทสนทนาสมมติ · Fictional demo profiles and chat")**, not just the caption. We'll send you labelled screen clips. Please use those instead of recording your own screen.
- Don't film your real account, other members' profiles, chats, People I Met choices or anything in Open to something more.

## Filming people at your table

- **Ask everyone at the table first, out loud, before you film.** If anyone says no, don't film them, and keep them out of frame or blurred.
- No faces, names, LINE IDs or workplaces of other members without their clear OK. Your own face is your call.
- If someone later asks you to take a clip down, take it down.
- Don't film at women-only or women and non-binary tables unless every person there agrees.

## Do

- Show the real place, the food, the walk there, the moment the table warms up.
- Say how it felt to go alone.
- Keep it in your own voice. We won't script you.

## Don't

- Use the BMA logo, or say the BMA made, runs or endorses Jurrgun.
- Show or invent numbers ("thousands of members", "everyone's on it"). There are none to quote yet.
- Call it a dating app, rate people or "rank your table".
- Use dashes in the Thai or English text of our tagline and lines.
- Post before the embargo lifts.
