# Jurrgun Brand Positioning

*2026-10-15. Settles the BMA question from the brand audit (`.agents/brand-audit.md`). Built from the owner's answers and `.agents/brand-context.md`.*

## The decision

**Jurrgun is an independent brand, run by its founder, made with BMA as its main client and endorser.**

- **Owner and operator:** the founder runs Jurrgun, in coordination with BMA and with other vendors, hosts and partners (dating and friend apps such as CMB and Tinder, event organisers, the private sector).
- **BMA:** the main client and the endorser. It is not the owner. BMA supplied the type system, so Sao Chingcha is used with permission.
- **Ambition:** to be both an official city service and an independent company. The endorsed-brand model allows both: BMA's backing gives legitimacy in Bangkok, and the independent brand can grow and partner beyond it.

In brand-architecture terms, this is an **endorsed brand**. Jurrgun is the name people know, and BMA appears as the endorser, the way a product says "by" or "with" a trusted parent. Jurrgun is never presented as BMA itself.

## 01 Category

- **Stated category:** social meetup app.
- **Actual category, the one people choose from:** dating apps (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge), friend apps (Bumble BFF), stranger dinners (Timeleft), groups (Meetup, Facebook groups, LINE OpenChat), and the default option: scrolling social media or staying in.
- **Opportunity category:** **human-first social life for the city.** It's an alternative to swiping and scrolling, not another app in either race.

**Recommendation:** don't compete as "a better dating app" or "a better events app". Own **"the human way to meet people in Bangkok"**: offline first, small groups, fair, and backed by the city.

## 02 Landscape

Two axes:

- **Screen-first ←→ Real-world first:** does the product keep you on your phone or get you to a table?
- **Engagement-driven ←→ Trust-driven:** does it earn by holding attention, or by being safe and accountable?

| Who | Where they sit | What they own |
|---|---|---|
| Tinder, Bumble, Hinge | Screen-first, engagement-driven | Romantic matching through browsing and swiping |
| Bumble BFF | Screen-first, engagement-driven | Friend matching by profile |
| Instagram, TikTok | Screen-first, engagement-driven | Feeling connected without meeting anyone |
| Timeleft | Real-world, commercial | One curated paid dinner with strangers |
| Meetup, Facebook groups, LINE OpenChat | Mixed, unvetted | Interest groups, with quality left to chance |
| BMA events, VisitBangkok | Real-world, trusted | Places and festivals, but no help meeting people there |

**The empty space:** real-world first *and* trust-driven *and* personally matched. City events have the trust but don't introduce you to anyone. Apps make introductions but are built to keep you scrolling. Jurrgun sits where the two meet.

## 03 Territory

- **The space:** meeting people the human way, at a real table in your own city, with nobody browsing or rejecting you.
- **The audience owned:** people in Bangkok, Thai or foreign, aged roughly 20–35, who are tired of swiping and scrolling and want more people in their life without the risk of meeting a stranger from an app.
- **The moat:** Jurrgun combines what neither side can copy alone. BMA's endorsement, public venues and official calendar give it legitimacy no startup can buy. Its independent operation and partner network (apps, venues, hosts) give it a speed and warmth no government service has. The rules are product mechanics, not slogans: fair small-group seating, mutual-only reveals, friends-first.

## 04 Positioning statement

**Framework:** For people in Bangkok who want real friends and connection without swiping or scrolling, Jurrgun is the human-first way to meet people that seats you in small, fairly matched groups at real places in the city, because it's built with the BMA, run independently with trusted hosts and partners, and designed so nobody is ever browsed, rejected or exposed.

**Strategic (internal):** Jurrgun is the endorsed social layer of Bangkok. BMA's backing makes it legitimate, independent operation makes it good to use, and the partner network (apps, venues, events, private sector) makes it full of things to do. We win the time people currently give to swiping and scrolling by making a real table easier and safer than another feed.

**Human (public):** Bangkok's friendliest way to meet people. Small groups, real places, no swiping. Made with the city, so you can just show up.

## 05 Proof points

1. **Backed by the city:** built with the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration, using its type system and its public places and official calendar.
2. **Nobody browses you:** no profiles to scroll and no cold messages. You only hear when the interest is mutual.
3. **Fair by design:** tables of 4 to 6, seated by a published fairness rule (stable matching), not by an engagement algorithm.
4. **Friends first:** romance stays off unless you turn it on, and it is only ever revealed when both people want it.
5. **Safe by default:** public venues, briefed hosts, check-in, a no-ghosting rule, and private data that no staff member can see.

## 06 What Jurrgun refuses to be

- Jurrgun is **not a dating app**. Dating partners plug in as a lane you opt into, never as the front door.
- Jurrgun will **never compete on how many people you can browse**, or rank anyone by desirability.
- Jurrgun is **not a government service**. It doesn't look or feel like a form, and BMA's name is an endorsement, not the brand.
- Jurrgun will **never sell attention or personal data**. Partners pay for good events, not for access to people.

## 07 In one sentence

**Jurrgun is Bangkok's human-first way to meet people: small groups at real city places, made with the BMA, so it's safe, fair and never about swiping.**

## What this changes in the product (each step for owner approval)

1. **Endorsement line** on the landing page, intro and footer, in wording BMA approves. Draft: TH "ร่วมกับกรุงเทพมหานคร", EN "Made with the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration". The privacy notice should name the operator (the data controller) separately from BMA.
2. **City Pulse framing:** "Anonymous totals go to the BMA to make the city easier to meet people in." This is honest about who receives the data, and only totals of 10 or more are shared.
3. **Partner lane:** partner apps and events (CMB, Tinder, organisers) appear clearly labelled as partners. Romance partners stay behind the "open to something more" opt-in.
4. **Typeface:** keep Sao Chingcha for headings. With BMA's permission, it is the visible sign of the endorsement.

## Red flags to watch

- **"Taxpayer-funded dating app" headlines.** Mitigation: friends-first by default, and dating partners kept behind the opt-in. Never lead with dating partners.
- **"BMA tracks your social life."** Mitigation: the operator is named, City Pulse publishes totals only, and there are no third-party trackers.
- **Partner logos crowding the brand.** Mitigation: Jurrgun stays the master brand, with BMA as endorser and partners labelled inside events.
