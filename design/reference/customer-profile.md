# Jurrgun Customer Profile and North Star

*2026-10-15. Built with the target-audience skill from `.agents/brand-context.md`, `design/reference/brand-positioning.md` and the PRD. There's no member data yet (pre-pilot), so this is a hypothesis to test in the pilot. Quotes are illustrative, written in the audience's language; replace them with real ones from pilot interviews.*

## The decision in one line

**Launch for "the Rebuilder": people aged 23 to 32 who moved to Bangkok in the last two years and are rebuilding a social life from zero.** That's people from other provinces and long-stay foreigners, together. Everyone is still welcome, but the first events, copy and channels are made for them.

Why them first:

- **Highest intent:** they feel the gap every weekend and are actively looking.
- **Fits the cold-start fix:** newcomers say yes to "show up alone" events, which is exactly the format that fills tables.
- **The app already serves them:** the "New to Bangkok" path and newcomer flag, language tables, the Getting around and New to Bangkok guides, Bangkok Buddy and the 50-district passport ("learn the city with someone").
- **Thai and foreign newcomers share the same problem,** which delivers the "Thai and expat in one community" promise without effort.
- **Easy to reach:** universities, coworking spaces, first jobs, DTV/long-stay communities, provincial alumni groups and BMA district offices.

## 01 Audience overview

The Rebuilder came to Bangkok for a first job, a transfer, a degree or a new life. The city is exciting, but their friends are back home or scattered. Weeknights end in a condo and a phone. Weekends are either alone at a café or tagging along with colleagues they don't really click with. They've tried the options. Dating apps feel like the only door, and they're tiring. Facebook groups and LINE OpenChat are loud and random. Paid stranger dinners are fun once but pricey and one-off. Showing up alone to a big event feels awkward. What they want is simple and hard to find: a few people they see again, in places that make Bangkok feel like theirs.

## 02 Primary audience profile

**Demographics**

- **Age:** 23 to 32, with the core at 25 to 29.
- **Gender:** any; matching never uses it.
- **Location:** central and inner Bangkok, along the BTS/MRT lines (Ari, Phaya Thai, Bang Sue, Sathon, Phra Khanong, On Nut, Huai Khwang, Lat Phrao). Many rent near work.
- **Income:** entry to mid salaries (15,000 to 50,000 THB a month). Price-sensitive: free or under 300 THB feels right.
- **Life stage:** 0 to 24 months in Bangkok. First or second job, a grad student or remote worker. Single or in a relationship. Mostly no kids.

**Psychographics**

- **Values:** belonging without pressure, being themselves, safety, fairness, trying new things, not wasting money.
- **Aspirations:** "a Bangkok that feels like mine". A regular table, a weekend crew, someone to call for Songkran.
- **Self-identity:** open but not loud. Curious about the city, a little shy alone, happy once a conversation starts.
- **Brands they like:** Grab and LINE (easy, local), Muji and Uniqlo (calm, honest), Timeleft (the idea), Duolingo (playful habit), Apple (clean), and local cafés with character.
- **Content:** TikTok and IG Reels (cafés, hidden spots, "things to do in Bangkok"), YouTube vlogs, Pantip, BKK lifestyle pages (Wongnai, The Cloud, Mango Zero), and English expat pages and subreddits for foreigners.
- **Communities:** university alumni groups, provincial "คนเชียงใหม่ในกรุงเทพฯ"-style groups, workplace LINE groups, run clubs, language exchanges, DTV and long-stay Facebook groups.

**Behaviour**

- **Decides by:** social proof and safety signals first, then "is it easy and cheap". They will try something once if a friend shares it or it looks official.
- **Triggers:**
  - a lonely weekend
  - a holiday they spend alone (Songkran, New Year)
  - a breakup
  - a friend leaving the city
  - a "you should get out more" moment
  - payday
- **Discovers through:** short video, friends' shares (LINE and IG stories), university or company channels, festival moments.
- **Buying process:** sees a post → checks it's safe and real → takes a quiz or browses an event → RSVPs to one free, low-pressure event → decides after the first table whether to come back.

## 03 Personas

### Ploy, 26: from Chiang Mai, UX designer, lives in Ari, 8 months in Bangkok

*"I love my job, but after six my whole social life is my phone. I don't want another dating app. I just want people to go to the market with."*

- **Her world:** she works hybrid in Phaya Thai, does weekend café-hopping alone and posts stories nobody replies to. Her university friends are in Chiang Mai. She speaks Thai, and English at work.
- **Goals:**
  - three or four people to meet most weekends
  - learn the old town properly
  - feel safe meeting strangers
- **Frustrations:**
  - dating apps keep pulling every chat toward dating
  - big events where she stands at the edge
  - plans that die in group chats
- **Needs from us:** small tables, someone who keeps the conversation going (a host), a reason to come back next week (her buddy, a quest), and that it's free.
- **Triggers:** a friend's story with her Bangkok Type, or a free Saturday morning market walk in Ari. She hesitates if it looks like dating or if she must go to a bar.
- **Channels:** IG Reels, TikTok, LINE OA, design-community Facebook groups.
- **How Jurrgun solves it:** a table of 4 to 6 at a real place with a host, then a buddy to explore the 50 districts with. No swiping, and dating stays off unless she switches it on.

### Daniel, 30: from Manila, remote marketing manager on a DTV visa, lives in On Nut, 5 months in Bangkok

*"Every expat thing is a bar night. I want Thai friends and to actually learn the language, not just hang out with other foreigners."*

- **His world:** works from a coworking space, goes to the gym, takes Thai lessons on an app. He knows a few expats but no locals. The language barrier makes him shy outside English.
- **Goals:**
  - Thai friends
  - real Thai practice
  - weekend trips around the city
- **Frustrations:**
  - English-only expat bubbles
  - nightlife as the default
  - apps that need a Thai phone number
- **Needs from us:** language tables with Thai speakers, English-friendly events, sign-up without a Thai SIM, and a clear code of conduct.
- **Triggers:** an English-friendly language table near the BTS, or a coworking or embassy newsletter. He hesitates if everything is in Thai or feels like a tourist product.
- **Channels:** expat Facebook groups, r/Bangkok, LinkedIn, coworking Slack and LINE, YouTube.
- **How Jurrgun solves it:** Thai and English tables are seated fairly so everyone shares a language, plus a newcomer path and a Thai buddy to learn the city with.

### Ton, 28: Bangkok-born, accountant, lives in Lat Phrao, friends married or moved away

*"I grew up here, but somehow I don't know anyone anymore. It's work, home, Netflix."*

- **Their world:** a home-work loop. Weekends with family. Their old friends have kids. They're quietly lonely and a little embarrassed about it.
- **Goals:**
  - a new crew
  - get back into badminton or running
  - something fun that isn't drinking
- **Frustrations:**
  - feeling too old to "make friends"
  - awkward one-on-one meetups
  - events full of people networking
- **Needs from us:** activity-first events (sport, food, walks), the same faces again, no pressure.
- **Triggers:** a free Sunday badminton table in their district, or a Songkran go-together event. They hesitate if it feels like a singles night.
- **Channels:** Facebook, LINE, YouTube, Pantip.
- **How Jurrgun solves it:** activity tables in their own district every week and a circle that meets again.

Ton is the secondary segment (a "Rebuilder in their own city"). Ploy and Daniel are the core.

## 04 Audience language

**Their problem:**

- "ย้ายมาทำงานกรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีเพื่อนเลย"
- "after work it's just me and my phone"
- "แอปหาเพื่อนสุดท้ายก็กลายเป็นแอปหาคู่"
- "I don't want to go to a bar alone"
- "everyone already has their group"

**What they want:**

- "มีแก๊งไปด้วยกันทุกเสาร์"
- "a regular table"
- "Thai friends, not just expats"
- "feel like this city is mine"
- "เจอกันอีกนะ"

**Why they chose competitors:**

- "Bumble BFF was the only option"
- "Timeleft was fun but 700 baht every time"
- "the Facebook group was easy to join"

**What this means for the brand:** lead with *"ย้ายมากรุงเทพฯ? เจอเพื่อนใหม่ที่โต๊ะเล็ก ๆ ใกล้บ้านคุณ"* / *"New in Bangkok? Make friends at a small table near you."* Use "alone" honestly: "come alone, everyone does". Never say "networking" or "singles".

## 05 Who Jurrgun is not for (at launch)

- **People who want dating first and only.** They can switch romance on, but we never market to them. Dating apps serve them better.
- **Nightlife and party seekers.** Our tables are activity and food first. Jurrgun never leads with alcohol.
- **Tourists on a short stay.** Eligibility is people living in Bangkok, and repeat tables need people who stay.
- **Professional networkers** looking for business leads. That's not the vibe, and it ruins tables.

## 06 Implications

- **Messaging:**
  - "come alone, leave with people to see again"
  - small tables, a host, free
  - the "New to Bangkok" angle
  - the Bangkok Type as the fun hook
  - "Made with the BMA" as the safety signal
- **Channels:**
  - IG and TikTok short video, built around the type cards and "my first table" stories
  - LINE OA for reminders and invites
  - universities and graduate programmes
  - coworking spaces
  - provincial alumni groups
  - DTV and expat groups
  - BMA district offices and festival booths (Songkran, Loy Krathong, Design Week)
- **Voice:** warm, Thai-first, a friend who knows the city. "Come along", not "register".
- **Product:** see the event programme and the journey below.

## North Star

**North Star metric: Weekly Reconnected Members.** These are members who sat at a table this week with at least one person they had met at Jurrgun before.

It is the one number that proves the mission (strangers becoming people you see again) rather than downloads, swipes or one-off attendance. It rises only when tables fill, people come back and connections are mutual. It also can't be gamed with scrolling.

**Inputs that drive it (the levers):**

| Input | Pilot target | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| New member → first RSVP | ≥ 40% | newcomer onboarding, type quiz, a "your first table" pick |
| RSVP → check-in | ≥ 70% | reminders, a buddy to go with, the no-show rule |
| Attended → mutual connection | ≥ 50% | hosts, small tables, People I Met |
| Connected → a second table together within 30 days | ≥ 35% | "Meet again" tables, circles, buddy quests |
| Felt safe | ≥ 90% | public venues, hosts, the code of conduct |

**Guardrails:** < 1 harassment report per 100 attendees, no-shows < 15%, and friends-only members never see dating.

The admin insights already count check-ins and connections. Weekly Reconnected Members is computable from registrations (checked in this week) joined to connections (made earlier). Add it to the admin dashboard as the headline number.

## Event programme for the Rebuilder

Recurring weekly slots, so cold start becomes a habit. Each one is free or under 300 THB, in a public venue near BTS/MRT, with a host and tables of 4 to 6.

| Slot | Format | Why it works for them | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat 9:00 | **New in Bangkok: morning market walk** (Ari, Or Tor Kor, Talat Phlu) | daytime, cheap, easy to talk while walking | newcomers, daytime, city_quest |
| Sat 15:00 | **Thai ↔ English language table** (TCDC, a café in Phaya Thai) | Daniel's top need; built-in topic | language_exchange, english_friendly, newcomers |
| Sun 8:00 | **Park run and coffee** (Benjakitti, Lumphini) | Ton's activity-first; no drinking | daytime, running |
| Wed 19:00 | **After-work board games** (a café near BTS) | beats the condo loop, low pressure | board_game, english_friendly |
| Monthly | **City Quest: old town with a host** (VisitBangkok routes) | learn the city; stamps for the passport | city_quest, newcomers |
| Festivals | **Go together**: Songkran, Loy Krathong, Design Week | the holidays newcomers dread spending alone | festival |
| Fortnightly | **Meet again table**: same crew, new place | drives the north star directly | |
| Opt-in only | **💘 Open to something more socials** (dating partners) | only for members who switched it on | open_to_more |

## The Rebuilder's journey through the site (what to build)

1. **Find:** a type card on IG or a friend's LINE share leads to `/types/<type>` (built), or a newcomer link leads to **`/new`**.
2. **Land on `/new`:** "ย้ายมากรุงเทพฯ? / New in Bangkok?" Come alone, small tables, a host, free, and Made with the BMA. Below that: this week's newcomer tables (live) and the quiz.
3. **Sign up and onboard:** pre-tick "I'm new to Bangkok" (newcomer flag), English or Thai, and email sign-up for people without a Thai SIM.
4. **First table pick:** after the quiz, "Your first table" suggests one newcomer-friendly event this week near their district.
5. **Show up:** reminders, the host, check-in, then People I Met.
6. **Come back:** a "Meet again" nudge, a buddy, a quest, a stamp. The north star moves.

Build order (owner approves): `/new` landing with live newcomer events → the first-table pick after the quiz → the "Meet again" event type and nudge → the Weekly Reconnected Members tile on the admin dashboard → seed the weekly slots as demo events.
