# Bangkok Type quiz: review (10-15)

*How the quiz feels today, and what to change so it reads like a person talking, not a form. Reviewed on a phone-sized screen (375 px) with a demo member, plus a count over the whole bank. Numbers: 47 scenarios, 752 answers.*

The quiz works: it's fair and accurate (design/reference/quiz-balance.md). What makes it feel robotic is mostly the **writing** and the **sameness**, not the maths.

## What feels robotic, and what to do

### 1. Every answer announces its letters
62% of answers (469 of 752) carry a stock phrase that signals Familiar or Discover: "your usual…", "the same faces every Wednesday", "same spot, same small group", "none of you has tried", "new to everyone". After three questions people notice the pattern and start picking a *letter*, not a moment.

- Before: "The morning tai chi circle at Lumphini Park, same spot, same small group"
- After: "Tai chi at Lumphini at 6am. The aunties already know your name"
- Before: "A Suk Sawat road food stall none of you has tried"
- After: "That stall on Suk Sawat everyone keeps posting. Finally"

**Rule:** show the letter through a detail, never by naming it. At most one in five answers per question may use "usual", "regular", "never" or "new".

### 2. Answers are too long to scan on a phone
Answers average 16 words; 82 run past 18. Six of them is about 100 words per question and 2,000 per quiz. On a phone you scroll to see all six, every time.

**Rule:** 6 to 12 words, one picture in your head. Put the place last, and only if it adds flavour.

### 3. Places are there to cover the map, not because they fit
Each bank file must use all 50 districts, so answers name places most people don't know ("the canal-side road along Khlong Thawi Watthana in Nong Khaem"). People then pick the place they recognise, not the answer that's them.

**Rule:** keep the 50-district coverage across the *whole* bank, not per file. Use a famous place where the scenario needs one; use an outer district where it's genuinely the best fit, with a detail that makes it vivid.

### 4. Some answers don't make sense for the question
To fit all four letters, some answers bend the situation:
- "One year with your partner": "A long table of friends at a stall none of you has tried" (an anniversary with a crowd at a random stall)
- "The storm just stopped" (a *solo* scenario): "Join a group walk through the Saen Saep canal communities"
- "Your parents come for the day": "A café nobody has posted yet, just you and your parents"

**Rule:** the sense check comes first. If a type has no believable answer to a scenario, the scenario is wrong for the bank; rewrite it.

### 5. Romance is in everyone's quiz
9 of 47 scenarios are dates ("One year with your partner", "A second date is happening this week"), and about 3 or 4 show up in every quiz unless the member is friends-only. That clashes with D4 and D10 (friends-first; romance is opt-in and never leads) and it's awkward for anyone single or not looking.

**Fix:** date scenarios only for members who chose 💘 Open to something more (Task Q5). Everyone else gets friends, family, solo, coworkers and city life.

### 6. Every question is the same screen
20 times: a question, a stock skyline with one emoji, six text cards. Nothing reacts to you, nothing is funny, and there's no sense of progress beyond "1 / 20".

**Fixes:** mixed formats (Task Q4); light, warm humour (Task Q3); small reactions between questions ("Same. The BTS at 6pm is a sport."); a halfway nudge at question 10 ("Halfway. You're leaning Chill so far."); art that matches the scenario (rain, market, river) instead of one skyline.

### 7. You have to pick even when nothing fits
There's Skip, but it reads as giving up. Forced picks add noise to the result.

**Fix:** a gentle "None of these are me" that counts as no vote, with a cap (say 3 per quiz).

### 8. The intro and result talk like a report
- Intro: "20 questions, tap to pick · About 3 min · Private". Accurate but flat. Try: "20 quick Bangkok moments. Go with your gut. About 3 minutes, and only you see your answers."
- Result: "Your blend 52% / 47% / 47%", "WING", "Close to…". Percentages that close look like the quiz couldn't decide, and "wing" is Enneagram jargon. Try: "Mostly a Park Regular, with a bit of Neighbourhood Keeper", and "why you got this" from their own picks (Task Q7).
- "Show my type to my groupmates" should say "to people at my Tables" (D5 naming).

### 9. The Thai reads like a translation
Thai answers follow the English word for word ("ที่ยังไม่มีใครโพสต์", "เพิ่มรอบนิดหน่อยเพราะหยุดยาว"). Per design/reference/thai-voice.md, write the Thai first in its own voice, then the English, rather than translating.
