# Record of processing activities · บันทึกรายการกิจกรรมการประมวลผล (PDPA มาตรา 39)

*Drafted 16 Oct 2026 from a read of `origin/main` at a038582, updated the same day for the privacy fixes (contact shares, research answers at deactivation, the data export, exit notes and unread notifications) (schema, routes, services, the privacy notice facts in `src/content/privacy.ts`). It is a working record for the owner, not legal advice; a Thai data protection lawyer should review it before the pilot. Unknown facts are listed under "Open items" at the end, never guessed. `test/compliance-docs.test.ts` fails if a retention constant in `src/services/retention.ts` is missing from this file.*

**How to read it.** One row per processing activity. "s" means a PDPA section: s19 consent, s24(3) contract (to provide the service the member signed up for), s24(5) legitimate interest, s24(6) legal obligation, s26 explicit consent for sensitive data (sex life, sexual orientation, health). Every retention period names the constant that enforces it. Where a row says "no period in code", the data is kept until the member removes it or the account is deleted.

**Common to every row.** Hosting, database and file storage: SV Cloud on Cloudflare (Workers, a Postgres-compatible database reached through `src/db.ts`, files through `src/storage.ts`). Staff roles: `bma_admin`, `moderator`, `insight_viewer`, `host`, `partner_admin`, `circle_head` (Table Host); member role `user` (`src/schema.ts` ROLES). Staff never read romance, relationship, gender or private-choice data (`redactSensitive` in `src/lib/records.ts`, `test/staff-privacy.integration.test.ts`). The retention job runs at most once per `RETENTION_INTERVAL_MS` (86,400,000 ms, 24 hours) from the hourly clock or `/admin`, and writes one audit row with the action `RETENTION_ACTION` ("retention.run").

## 3. The controller · ผู้ควบคุมข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล

The privacy notice names "the Jurrgun team" as the data controller and the BMA as host, moderator and overseer. ## 4. DPO assessment · เจ้าหน้าที่คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล (มาตรา 41)

Section 41 requires a DPO when the core activity is (a) regular and systematic monitoring on a large scale, or (b) processing section 26 data. The PDPC's 2023 DPO notification sets large scale at 100,000 or more data subjects. Jurrgun has no members yet, so (a) is not met today. On (b), dating intents and Open to something more data are section 26 data, but they are an opt-in side of a friends-first service, so whether they make a "core activity" is a legal judgement. Recommendation: name a privacy lead now, and decide on a DPO with a lawyer and the BMA before the pilot opens dating to the public. This is an open item.

## 6. Cross-border transfers · การส่งข้อมูลไปต่างประเทศ (มาตรา 28 และ 29)

The notice (`TRANSFERS`) says data may be sent or stored outside Thailand: Cloudflare's network worldwide, and the email senders, push services and Esri in the United States, under each provider's standard data protection terms. The safeguard relied on today is the providers' terms; whether these meet the PDPC's 2023 transfer notifications (appropriate safeguards or a listed exception) is an open item for the lawyer, as is the database's home region.

## 7. Security summary · มาตรการรักษาความปลอดภัย (มาตรา 37(1))

- Passwords: PBKDF2-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and a salt; session ids, email addresses, codes, IPs and usernames stored only as hashes or HMACs.
- Secrets live only in SV Cloud settings, never in the repo (SVAGENTS.md rule 4).
- Consent gating: no consent row means no (`hasConsent`, `consentsFor`); sensitive data saved only while its consent is granted.
- Staff privacy: `redactSensitive` on audit write and display; staff pages select fixed columns; `test/staff-privacy.integration.test.ts`.
- k-anonymity: `K_THRESHOLD` = 10 for admin totals, `HOST_K` = 5 for a host's own events.
- Least data: Google and LINE ask for `openid` only; no GPS; sign-up source cut to a slug.
- Retention job with an audit row per run; the promises are tested in `test/privacy-promises.test.ts`.
- Breach handling: `design/reference/breach-response-plan.md`.
