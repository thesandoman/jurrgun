## 01 Strategy

**Launch type:** a **new brand, phased soft launch**. A closed pilot in 4 districts comes first. Then one public moment at **Loy Krathong**, the festival people least want to spend alone. City-wide comes later, once the pilot proves people come back. Cold start (R6) is the top risk, so every launch action has to fill a specific table, not just build awareness.

**Narrative:** Bangkok has millions of people, and too many of us still eat alone, especially if we're new here. Jurrgun gets you to a real table: 4 to 6 people, a real place in the city, a host, and a reason to meet again. It's independent, hosted and overseen by the BMA, so it's safe to just show up.

**Primary audience:** the Rebuilder, aged 23 to 32 and new to Bangkok within two years, Thai or foreign (`customer-profile.md`). The front doors are `/new` and `/international`.

**Pilot districts (proposal):**
- Phaya Thai (Ari)
- Ratchathewi (Victory Monument)
- Bang Rak (Charoen Krung, TCDC)
- Khlong Toei and Watthana (Benjakitti, Phrom Phong)

Phra Nakhon hosts the City Quests. They're all on BTS or MRT, near first jobs and condos, and they have BMA venues or friendly cafés.

**Goals for the 8-week pilot:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **Weekly Reconnected Members** (north star), by week 8 | **≥ 60 a week** |
| Members (pilot) | 300 to 500 |
| Registration → first RSVP | ≥ 40% |
| RSVP → check-in | ≥ 70% |
| Table fill (RSVPs / seats) | ≥ 60% |
| Checked in → mutual connection | ≥ 50% |
| "I felt safe" (4 to 5 out of 5) | ≥ 90% |
| Harassment reports | < 1 per 100 attendees |
| Pieces of UGC from attendees | ≥ 50 |

## 02 Pre-launch (now to 6 November, 3 weeks)

### Week -3: inside first (15 to 21 Oct)
- **The BMA and the team see it first.** Show film 01 (revised, 2:00, for BMA executives) to the BMA steering contact, the district champions in the 4 pilot districts, and the three admins.
  - Ask the BMA: the pilot districts, BMA venues (parks, libraries, TCDC, district halls), and named BMA moderators. Give them `moderator` or `insight_viewer` roles (see `staff-access.md`).
- **Brief everyone in one page** using the brand context, the Thai voice guide and staff access:
  - what Jurrgun is and isn't (friends-first, not a dating app);
  - how to describe the BMA ("an independent app, hosted and overseen by the BMA");
  - the Regular Table and Table Host names.
- **Recruit 12 to 16 Table Hosts.** Mix university alumni, run-club leads, language-exchange organisers, an LGBTQ+ community partner and two expat long-stayers. Each host commits to one fortnightly Regular Table for 8 weeks. Hosts are approved through `/circles/lead`.

### Week -2: assets ready (22 to 31 Oct)
- [ ] Re-rendered films from the re-render chat (all five), plus a 6 s bumper and a 1:1 cut of the promo for LINE
- [ ] Social profiles: IG, TikTok, a LINE OA (with rich menu: Tables this week · Take the quiz · New to Bangkok · From abroad), Facebook page. Same bio everywhere: "กลุ่มเล็ก สถานที่จริง แล้วเจอกันนะ · Small groups. Real Bangkok places. See you there."
- [ ] Share cards (done) and the quiz as the waitlist (done)
- [ ] Press and partner kit:
  - mark files
  - a one-pager in TH and EN
  - how matching works (fairness, mutual-only, friends-first)
  - the BMA role line and the safety summary
  - founder bio
  - 6 stills from real pilot tables (added after the soft launch)
- [ ] Seed the weekly programme in production: the newcomer slots in `customer-profile.md`, run by the Table Hosts, at least 3 tables a week per district
- [ ] Production check: health green, migrations applied, staff roles set, the privacy notice names the DPO (still open)

### Week -1: warm-up (1 to 6 Nov)
- **Closed beta, 60 to 100 people.** Friends of hosts, two university alumni groups, one coworking space, one expat group. Each brings one friend, using the bring-a-friend invite.
- **Teasers:** reel 04 ("มีเพื่อนใหม่ครั้งล่าสุดเมื่อไหร่?") and type-card shares ("I'm The Food Hunter 🍜, what's yours?") on the founder's and hosts' own accounts.
- **Creators, under embargo:** 6 to 8 nano and micro creators (1k to 50k followers) who are newcomers themselves: a provincial grad, a DTV expat, a Pride community voice. Each attends a closed-beta table alone and posts after soft launch day. Required in every post: friends-first, the tagline in the caption, and #ร่วมกับJurrgun / #ad. No BMA logo.

## 04 Public moment: Loy Krathong week (about 24 November [verify])

The festival people least want to spend alone, and a natural BMA moment.

**"ลอยกระทงด้วยกัน · Go together" tables.** Small groups meet an hour before at a BMA venue, then go together, with a host and a Bangkok Passport stamp.
- **Places:** Lumphini Park, Benjakitti, Chatuchak Park and the river piers in Phra Nakhon [verify the venues with the BMA].
- **Coverage:** at least 12 tables, 4 to 6 people each, across the pilot districts.

**On BMA channels** (BMA's Facebook, LINE, district pages and VisitBangkok, with BMA approval):
- the promo, plus the plain reel (no pun);
- **film 02** as the feature film for Open to something more (owner, D9). Its message is that the opt-in exists and stays private. It never leads with dating, and its love-red moments sit beside the green.

**Press:** pitch the story "Bangkok's festival nobody should spend alone" to Thai lifestyle media (The Cloud, Mango Zero, Wongnai) and expat media, with stills from the soft launch tables.

**After the festival:** "Meet again" Regular Tables for everyone who went (north-star driver).

## 08 Risks during launch

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| Empty tables (cold start) | Fixed weekly slots, hosts commit 8 weeks, beta brings friends, merge thin tables 24 h before |
| Framed as a "taxpayer-funded dating app" | Friends-first in every public post. Film 02 only as a clearly opt-in feature. The BMA line says host and oversight |
| A safety incident | Public venues, hosts briefed, check-in, staff moderators on rota during event hours, report flow |
| Monsoon or PM2.5 | Indoor backups for every outdoor table (TCDC, libraries, cafés) |
| Privacy questions | `staff-access.md`, a privacy notice that names the DPO, no trackers |
